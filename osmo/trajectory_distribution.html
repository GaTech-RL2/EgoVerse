Generated trajectory distribution

MimicGen expansion over the 512x512 PushShapes workspace · object paths, not agent paths

Every trajectory here was generated: a handful of scripted seed demos retargeted onto random layouts, replayed, and kept only when coverage cleared 0.95. The question these plots answer is whether that expansion actually covers the workspace, or just re-runs the seeds in place.

Where the object travels

Occupancy of the object's centre over every generated episode, on a 32x32 grid. Darker = more visits. Blank cells were never reached.

Start → goal spread

One mark per episode: where the object began (hollow) and the goal it was driven to (filled). Lines connect the pairs, so a dense fan means the seeds generalised across the arena rather than repeating one displacement.

Object paths

Up to 70 object trajectories per embodiment, drawn end to end.
