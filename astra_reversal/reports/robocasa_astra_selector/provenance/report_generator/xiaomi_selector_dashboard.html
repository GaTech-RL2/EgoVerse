What was verified

Paired reset accounting

One physical episode includes an explicit restore and all controls until success or horizon. A 16-action chunk continues that episode without a reset. Constructor, anchor-selection and diagnostic restores are separate overhead, excluded from SR.

Teacher accounting

Counts include the Codex harness. One CLI job is not necessarily one provider request; internal retries are unobserved. Token usage is not a verified dollar charge for a ChatGPT plan. Actual served model identity is unavailable.

Checkpoint, task settings and comparison limits
The released Xiaomi-Robotics-1 RoboCasa365 weights and model factory remain frozen. The original processor uses three cameras, four historical frames sampled every two controls, a 95% crop, a 14D state padded to 60 dimensions and five native flow evaluations. The released action decoder produces 12 channels and executes 16 controls per prediction.
PackIdenticalLunches uses a 3,900-control horizon; ArrangeBreadBasket uses 4,350. Kitchens use the pretrain split. The five lunch cases are a seen composite task; the bread-basket case is a held-out composite. This is a small selected cohort, not all OOD tasks.
Old qualification videos have the same nominal task/seed identity, but their exact model XML equivalence to these new anchors is uncertified. Current native and guided attempts must pass the registered saved-reset checks. Earlier starts required exact image equality; the final continuation permits a maximum one-level channel difference at no more than 64 pixels per 256 × 256 camera. Physical fingerprints, non-camera observations and instructions still require exact equality. No camera values are replaced or edited by this verification. Neither comparison restores the old persistent random-noise trajectory.
Text donors are online subgoals. Vision donors are perturbations of current observations or the preceding review frame. This experiment does not retrieve training demonstrations, learn a skill library or update policy weights.
