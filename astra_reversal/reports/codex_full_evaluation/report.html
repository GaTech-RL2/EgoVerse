What VEI and VLI change

Mixing visual representations with a verified donor library.

__vision-flow.svg__
Evaluation resetSeed 47 · state 0 · Euler-10 · five actions per replan · 300 actions per physical rollout
Reasoner cadenceEvery 25 actions · at most 12 decisions per rollout · two rescue revisions
Frozen donor library45 paired external/wrist frames · nine standard training tasks · no OOD demonstration donors

VEI interpolates post-projector visual tokens on the same patch grid. VLI interpolates visual slots at fixed blocks 0–16; Astra selects the donor and mixing strength, not the blocks. Both preserve the target instruction. No donor proprioception or direct text-slot writes are used. The bank contains 45 paired frames from nine standard training demonstrations/tasks. Random controls use the same donor catalog with uniform mixing strength and matched policy noise; their deferral distribution is not matched to Astra.

Reading limits and evidence boundaries
  • Partial results reflect tasks whose sealed evidence was available and passed audit. Completion order and early native successes can bias this subset. They are descriptive counts, not full-cohort estimates or evidence of statistical superiority.
  • The suite contains 20 Goal-OOD and Spatial-OOD compositions. Training overlap of the pretrained checkpoint is unknown. This is frozen-policy inference evaluation, not a claim about reinforcement-learning sample efficiency or held-out-task generalization.
  • “Calls” counts confirmed started Codex CLI jobs, not internal provider HTTP requests. Internal retry counts and the actual served model are not exposed by these receipts. The requested configuration is gpt-6-astra with medium reasoning effort.
  • Reported token totals use input plus output where a total was not directly provided. Reasoning-token counts are a subset of output tokens. Unknown or incomplete usage stays unknown or a lower bound; no price conversion is inferred from a ChatGPT plan.
  • Media selection is deterministic: first audited identity/reset, followed by the first native-baseline failure in identity order when available. Selection does not inspect assisted outcomes. These are examples, not an independent success sample.
  • Videos are recorded at 20 frames/s and omit inference waits. Frames precede executed actions; a terminal observation after the last action may not appear. Video duration is not measured wall-clock latency. Raw images come from the first policy observation after reset stabilization.
  • Archives were sealed with arrays, events, provider evidence and fresh frozen-weight receipts. Independent audits check array semantics and join Codex receipts to local jobs. This report rechecks receipt and selected-media bindings; it does not replace those auditors.
